Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Who we are

Recrova Talent (https://recrovatalent.github.io) is a hiring website where people and companies connect, share updates and find jobs and candidates.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name (or company name), email address and password. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and are not readable by us.
  • If you sign in with Google or Facebook: the name, email address and profile picture those services share with us. We do not receive your password for those services.
  • Information you add: your profile (headline, about, experience, photo and similar details), posts, comments, jobs you post, and messages you send on the site.

How we use it

We use this information to run your account, show your profile and posts to other users, let people message each other, and show jobs and candidates. Profile information and posts you publish can be seen by other users of the site.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal data.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with our service providers: Supabase (database and sign-in) and GitHub Pages (website hosting). Google and Facebook process your data when you choose to sign in with them.

Your choices

You can edit your profile and delete your posts on the site. To ask for a copy of your data or any correction, email us at the address below.

Data deletion instructions

To delete your account and your data, email recrovatalents@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign up, with the subject "Delete my Recrova Talent account". We will delete your account, profile, posts and messages after confirming the request. If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove Recrova Talent from your Facebook settings under Apps and Websites, and then send us the email above to delete the data we hold.

Contact

Questions about this policy: recrovatalents@gmail.com.

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
